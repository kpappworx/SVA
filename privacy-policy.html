Who operates SVA

SVA: SelfCare AI is operated by KPAPPWORX TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED, India, using the brand KPAppWorx (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the SVA app, these support pages and information you send us for support.

Information you provide

You can record mood, energy and stress ratings; topics on your mind; optional check-in notes; goals; wake and wind-down times; workdays; routine preferences; and reminder choices. You can also record journal and gratitude entries, evening reflections, a focus intention and feedback on how you feel after a routine. SVA does not require a SVA account.

What SVA stores

SVA saves your choices and entries, timestamps, daily plans, routine activity and feedback, summaries, weekly reflections and hidden-pattern choices in local app storage. It works out patterns from your entries. It also keeps a smaller local snapshot for widgets and Siri Shortcuts, and caches whether SVA Pro is active.

With Health permission, saved information can include sleep duration with a morning check-in, step totals for a completed walk, and Health-derived wording in plans, summaries and widget information. The app does not copy your entire Apple Health record.

On-device processing and backups

The app calculates plans and patterns on your device. It has no SVA account server or app-managed cloud-sync service in this implementation. Your wellness entries are not uploaded to a KPAppWorx server by the app.

Local storage does not mean that no copy can leave your device. Depending on device settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup. Apple Health has its own storage and sync settings. Exports and information you choose to share are handled by the destination you choose.

Before publication: Resolve and test backup handling for personal health information, including derived data and widget snapshots, against Apple’s HealthKit requirements. The inspected app does not explicitly exclude these files from backup.

How AI and recommendations work

Built-in rules choose routines using your check-in, preferences, available Health context and, for Pro, feedback on routines that have helped you. The recommendations are not a medical assessment.

On supported devices, when enabled, SVA uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework to word short daily, evening and weekly summaries. It gives the model a short set of facts, such as mood, energy, stress, activity completion and comparisons with usual sleep or movement. It does not give this model your free-text notes, journal entries or raw Health samples. SVA does not send these prompts to an external AI provider.

SVA checks generated wording and falls back to built-in text when the model is unavailable or a response fails its checks. These checks cannot guarantee accuracy. You can turn off “Write summaries with Apple Intelligence” in You → Privacy & data. The app contains no process that trains a model on your entries.

Separately, SVA checks notes and journal text on-device for certain crisis-related phrases to show a support screen. This is a limited automated check, not continuous monitoring, a diagnosis or an emergency response service.

Optional Apple Health access

Apple Health access is optional. SVA works without it. With your permission, it reads sleep, step counts, exercise minutes and mindful sessions to provide context and personalise suggestions. It compares available sleep and movement information with your own recent history.

If you allow writes, SVA saves check-in mood and evening mood as State of Mind, including relevant selected associations, and breathing routines as mindful sessions. Change each read or write permission in the Health app’s access settings for SVA. Revoking permission stops permitted future access; it does not erase copies already saved in SVA or Health.

The app uses Health information for your self-care features, not advertising, sale to data brokers or external AI processing. Health entries written by SVA remain in Apple Health when you delete SVA’s local information; manage those records separately in Health.

Apple services, websites and support

Purchases, restores and subscription management communicate with Apple through StoreKit. SVA reads verified transaction and entitlement information to determine Pro access; it does not receive your card details. Apple may handle platform diagnostics and service information under your Apple settings and its own policies.

Widgets can display your mood, plan or patterns on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. Siri Shortcuts can return the next routine and its reason to Siri. Consider who can see or hear that information and review your device’s widget, Siri and notification settings. Reminders and timer notices are scheduled locally.

The inspected app has no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. This is separate from Apple’s platform services and the external services you choose to use.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. Email is processed through the email providers involved, including Gmail for our published support address. Please avoid sending health records, journal exports or unnecessary sensitive information.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to GitHub. The pages add no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or tracking cookies. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External websites have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

SVA does not automatically erase your wellness history after a fixed period. The free plan’s 14-day visible history is a viewing limit, not a deletion schedule. Older records remain in local storage and exports. If a saved file cannot be read, the app may preserve a local recovery copy.

In SVA, open You → Privacy & data → Delete everything, then confirm “Delete all SVA data”. This requests removal of the local wellness files, including recovery and widget files, and clears scheduled and delivered notifications. It does not cancel a subscription or erase Apple Health records, Apple purchase records, existing backups or exports you shared elsewhere. The Pro-status cache is separate from wellness history.

Export my data creates a local JSON file; Share export file lets you send it to a destination you choose. Temporary export files are not covered by the current Delete everything action. Delete any exported copies and backups separately through the service or device that holds them.

Before publication: The current delete action suppresses file-deletion errors; asynchronous writes also need deletion-race testing. Resolve and verify these issues before promising reliable complete deletion. Confirm the actual retention and deletion rules for support emails; no support-mail retention period has been established in this audit.

Security

Local wellness and widget files use iOS Data Protection and atomic file writes. They are configured to become available after the first device unlock following a restart. This is not a promise of end-to-end encryption or protection against every risk. Keep your device secured and updated, and protect exports, which contain readable personal information. The public pages use HTTPS.

Children’s privacy

SVA is a wellness tool, not a medical or emergency-care service. If you have a concern about a child’s information, contact kpappworx@gmail.com.

Before publication: The owner and qualified legal counsel must confirm the intended age range, applicable parental-consent requirements and actual age safeguards. The code has no verified age or parental-consent flow. Do not publish an invented age threshold or an “all ages” assurance.

International processing

Core wellness processing takes place on your device. Email support, GitHub-hosted pages and Apple services may involve processing outside your country, including outside India, under the relevant service’s arrangements. We do not promise that every service or backup stays in India.

Before publication: Confirm support-service arrangements, distribution territories and any legally required transfer safeguards or notices before publication.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can change preferences, turn off optional features, review Health permissions, hide patterns, export your data and request local deletion in the app. Hiding a pattern does not delete the entries used to calculate it.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing, obtain a copy, or complain to the relevant authority. Contact kpappworx@gmail.com about information you have sent us. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase wellness information held only in your app. Do not send your journal or Health record to establish a request.

Before publication: Counsel should confirm applicable rights, lawful bases, response processes and any required company address or grievance-contact details.
