Frequently asked questions

What is SVA?

SVA: SelfCare AI is a self-care and wellness app from KPAppWorx. It helps you check in with yourself, choose small daily actions and reflect on what seems to help.

How does SVA help with daily self-care?

It offers routines for breathing, walking, stretching, water, focus, journaling, gratitude and winding down. You can follow your daily plan, skip or swap supported suggestions, or start a quick action.

How do check-ins work?

Choose your mood and rate energy and stress. Topics and written notes are optional. Tap Build my plan or Update my plan. An evening reflection lets you note how the day felt and what helped.

How are plans and AI summaries generated?

Plans are chosen by rules on your device using your check-in, preferences and optional Health context. Pro can also use your routine feedback to tune suggestions. Apple Intelligence optionally words short summaries from structured facts on supported devices. When unavailable or off, built-in wording is used. Journal text is not sent to the summary model.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free includes check-ins, daily plans, all routines, evening reflection, one available pattern and the latest 14 days of visible history. Pro adds all available patterns, routine-feedback-based plan tuning, weekly reflections and full visible history. Patterns and reflections need enough entries; results are not guaranteed after a particular number of days. Export and deletion are available without Pro. Older history remains stored even without Pro.

Does SVA provide medical advice?

No. SVA offers general self-care guidance, not diagnosis, treatment, medical monitoring or emergency care. AI and pattern observations may be wrong. Ask a qualified professional before making medical decisions. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services; do not wait for SVA or email support.

Does SVA require an internet connection?

Core check-ins, routines, rule-based plans and local history do not need a SVA server. Purchases, restores, online support pages and email require connectivity. Apple Intelligence availability depends on your device and Apple settings, and model downloads may need internet access. Apple services and backups have their own connectivity needs.

How does optional Apple Health integration work?

If you choose Connect Apple Health, you can permit reads of sleep, steps, exercise minutes and mindful sessions, and writes of State of Mind and breathing sessions. Denying access does not stop core self-care features. Change permissions in Health’s access settings for SVA. The app cannot distinguish denied read access from missing data, so some context may simply not appear.

How does SVA protect my information?

Wellness records are stored locally with iOS Data Protection. Apple Intelligence summaries run on-device. Backups, exports, Health, Siri, purchases and email have separate implications; read the Privacy Policy. Widgets and Siri may display or speak personal context.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Use You → SVA Pro → Manage subscription when Pro is active, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SVA. Use Apple’s cancellation control. Deleting SVA or its information does not cancel billing. For a free or discounted trial, Apple advises cancelling at least 24 hours before it ends if you do not want renewal. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.

How do I restore purchases?

Use You → SVA Pro → Restore purchases, or Restore on the paywall. Be signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase and connect to the internet. If access does not return, check your subscription in Apple Settings and contact support with the issue and app version. Do not send payment-card details.

What subscriptions and trials are available?

The app is configured to request monthly and annual Pro products. The price and offer available to your Apple Account are shown by the App Store at purchase. Do not assume you are eligible for a trial. Trial and live product details still require confirmation before this draft is published.

How do I request a refund?

For purchases billed by Apple, follow Apple’s refund instructions. Eligibility and outcomes depend on Apple’s process and applicable law. Cancelling and requesting a refund are separate actions.

How can I export or delete my information?

Open You → Privacy & data → Export my data and then Share export file. To request removal of local wellness records, choose Delete everything and confirm Delete all SVA data. Apple Health records, exports, backups and purchases are separate; see the deletion details and current limitations. Do not reinstall as a first troubleshooting step: you may lose local history.

What if something is not working?

Check for an app update, close and reopen SVA, and note the steps that trigger the problem. For purchases, check your connection and Apple Account. For missing Health context, review permissions and available records. For unavailable AI summaries, review Apple Intelligence settings; built-in wording remains available.

How can I contact support?

Email kpappworx@gmail.com with the app version, device/iOS version and a short description. Redact private information in screenshots. Support email is not an emergency service.
